## What is a good prayer before starting to work?

A good prayer before starting to work is short, specific, and said out loud before you touch anything else on your phone. Here is one you can use tomorrow morning:

*Lord, before I begin this work, I offer it to You. Guard my words, keep me honest, and let what I do today serve You and the people in front of me. Amen.*

Use it at your desk, in the car before you drive, or standing in the kitchen. It takes about 30 seconds.

### A 30-second prayer before starting work

Say it before opening email, not after. The order matters: the prayer sets the intention, the inbox follows it.

### Why short and specific beats long and generic

A prayer you actually say daily beats a beautiful one you skip. ## What Catholic prayer can I say before work?

Catholics have a short traditional prayer for the start of the workday: an offering of the day's labour to God. It takes under a minute to pray out loud, which matters if you are trying to build a streak of faithfulness instead of another abandoned morning routine. ### The traditional Catholic prayer before work, full text

> O Lord, in union with the divine intention of Jesus Christ, I offer You all the prayers, works, and sufferings of this day, for the intentions of Your Sacred Heart, in union with the Holy Sacrifice of the Mass throughout the world, in reparation for my sins, for the intentions of my family and friends, and for the intentions of the Holy Father. Amen.

The Morning Offering exists in several wordings; this one is the common traditional form. Pray it out loud before you check email. The point is not the exact words but that the day is given to God before it is given to anyone else. That ordering is why a prayer before starting to work works as a daily anchor rather than an occasional act of devotion.

### A shorter version for the parking lot

> Jesus, I offer You this day, my work and my rest. Amen.

This one-liner fits the gap between turning off the ignition and walking through the door. Say it slowly, once, out loud.

## Is there an Orthodox prayer before starting work?

Yes. Orthodox tradition treats the first words of the morning as an offering, and the [Greek Orthodox Archdiocese of America](https://www.goarch.org/) publishes the standard morning prayers used in homes across North America, including Canada. The core move is simple: before you touch email or the news, you give the day to God out loud.

### The Orthodox morning offering, full text

Pray this while still in bed or standing before your icons:

> O Lord, grant me to meet this day in peace. Help me in all things to rely on Your holy will. In every hour of the day, reveal Your will to me. Whatever news comes to me this day, teach me to accept it with a calm heart, knowing that all is subject to Your holy will.

Then close with: Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner. Amen.

## How do I pray for guidance before work when I'm anxious?

Anxiety before work usually comes from one specific thing: a conversation you're dreading, a deadline, a decision you can't see past. Pray for guidance about that thing directly, not about "the day" in general. Say this prayer before starting to work, out loud if you can:

*Lord, I am anxious about this morning, and You know exactly why. I give You this worry by name before I carry it one step further. Give me wisdom for the decisions in front of me today. Give me the words to say and the restraint to hold my tongue when I need to. Order my steps, calm my mind, and let me work this day as Your servant, not as a slave to my fear. Amen.*

### Naming the specific worry out loud

"By name" is the hinge. Praying "Lord, help me with everything today" gives anxiety nowhere to go; praying "Lord, I'm afraid of the meeting at ten" puts the worry where you can hand it over. If you can't name it yet, pray until you can.

## What prayer fits a nurse or hospital worker starting a shift?

Hospital work starts before you reach the ward, so the prayer has to be short enough for a stairwell and honest about what the shift holds.

### A prayer before work in hospital, full text

*Lord, before I put on my badge, I put this shift in Your hands. Steady my hands, guard my words, and let me see each patient as You see them. Where I am rushed, make me patient. Where I am tired, be my strength. Commit to You the work of my hands this day. Amen.*

The closing line echoes Proverbs 16:3, "Commit to the Lord whatever you do, and he will establish your plans" ([Bible Gateway](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Proverbs+16%3A3&version=NIV)).

### Praying between the parking lot and the ward

You do not need a quiet room. Pray it out loud in the elevator or sitting in the car before the engine goes off; speaking it takes under thirty seconds and keeps your phone in your pocket instead of your face. ## Is there a special Friday morning prayer before work?

### Why Friday carries its own weight

Friday holds a particular place in Christian practice because it commemorates the day of Christ's crucifixion. Many believers mark it by small acts of devotion, abstinence, or extra prayer, which makes a Friday morning prayer before work a natural extension of that tradition. For someone who has lapsed from a daily quiet time, Friday is a low-pressure day to restart: the date itself reminds you why you are praying.

### A Friday morning prayer before work, full text

Lord Jesus Christ, You gave Your life on a Friday out of love for me. As I begin this day's work, let my hands do honest labour and my words bring peace. Keep me faithful in small tasks, patient under pressure, and mindful of those I serve. When I grow tired, remember Your sacrifice and strengthen me. I offer this day's work to You. Amen.

Pray it out loud before you check a single message, and let the prayer set the order of the morning.

## FAQ

### Can I pray before work in my own words, or does it have to be a written prayer?

Your own words are fine. Written texts exist because they give you something to say on mornings when you have nothing, not because God requires them. A good pattern is to start with a written prayer until it becomes habit, then let your own words grow out of it. What matters is that you actually pray before the day starts.

### What is the shortest prayer I can say before starting a shift?

One line: "Jesus, I offer You this day, my work and my rest. Amen." It fits the gap between turning off the ignition and walking through the door, and it takes about five seconds. If even that feels long on a bad morning, "Lord, I give You this shift" still counts. Short and said beats long and skipped every time.

### Is it okay to pray silently at my desk before starting work in an office?

Yes. Silence works, especially in a shared office where speaking aloud is awkward. The tradeoff is that spoken prayer tends to focus attention better and feels more deliberate, which is why the prayers in this guide are meant to be said out loud when you can. If your desk is public, pray in the car, the elevator, or the stairwell instead.

### How do I remember to pray before work when I grab my phone first thing?

Anchor the prayer to the alarm, because that is the one fixed point in an office morning. If your alarm has a dismiss button, you will usually dismiss it and scroll. ### Do I need to pray before work at all if I already did morning devotions?

If your devotions already happen before work and include offering the day to God, you have covered it. The problem this guide addresses is the gap between waking and working, where most lapsed routines break. A short prayer before starting to work is not a second devotion; it is a thirty-second bridge that carries the morning's intention into the tasks ahead.
